No slides about pricing theory. You bring a brief you need to quote, and you leave with the quote.

  • Scope it: break the brief into the parts a client is paying for
  • Price it: put a number on each part, with a buffer for revisions

Questions

Before you book

Do I need a project to bring?
It helps a lot. If you don't have one right now, bring the last project you quoted. You'll see what you'd change.
Is it recorded?
Yes. Everyone who registers gets the recording and the worksheet the next morning, whether they made it live or not.
I charge hourly. Is this still for me?
Yes. Most people who come start there. The workshops show you how to turn hours into parts a client can see and agree to pay for.
What if I can't make it?
Move to any later workshop, no charge. Write to us before the session starts.
