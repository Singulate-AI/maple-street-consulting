About

A small team that has quoted a lot of client work

Maple Street started as a Friday lunch where a few independent consultants traded notes on what they charged. The questions were always the same: what's my rate, how do I price a project, and how do I say the number out loud.

Now we teach it. Every session is built around your real work, and you leave with a price you can stand behind.
