About reviews

Is a review better than a workshop?
It's different. A workshop teaches the method on one project. A review applies it to your whole business: your rates, your packages and your next proposal.
Will you tell me what to charge?
We'll tell you what your numbers say, and what we'd change. The decision stays yours.
Is what I share confidential?
Yes. Your numbers and proposals stay between you and us, and we never use them as examples.
