How the 90 minutes go

Scope it, price it, say it

  1. Minutes 0 to 25

    Scope it

    Break your brief into the parts a client is paying for. Most people find one part they've been giving away.

  2. Minutes 25 to 60

    Price it

    Put a number on each part with the rate sheet, and add the buffer for revisions before the client asks for them.

  3. Minutes 60 to 90

    Say it

    Practice the sentence that goes with the number, in pairs. You'll say it out loud before you ever send it.
